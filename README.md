# MaiD Support

**MaiD** is a focused, native Markdown editor for macOS — side-by-side live preview, an
automatic outline from your headings, native Mermaid diagrams, and one-click HTML/PDF export.
No Electron, no account, no network access.

This is the place to **report a bug** or **request a feature**. Everything here is public, so
you can search existing reports, follow progress, and back ideas you like with a 👍.

## 🐞 Report a bug

➡️ **[Open a bug report](https://github.com/Australware/MaiD/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)**

To help us fix it fast, please include:

- Your **macOS version** — e.g. macOS 14.5 (Sonoma)
- Your **MaiD version** — find it in **MaiD ▸ About MaiD**
- **What happened**, what you expected instead, and the **steps to reproduce**
- A screenshot or a small sample `.md` file, if it helps

## 💡 Request a feature

➡️ **[Request a feature](https://github.com/Australware/MaiD/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)**

Tell us what you're trying to do and why it's awkward today — the problem you're hitting is
more useful to us than a specific solution.

## 🔎 Before you post

Please **[search existing issues](https://github.com/Australware/MaiD/issues)** first. If your
bug or idea is already there, add a comment or a 👍 rather than opening a duplicate — it helps
us see what matters most.

## 📨 Other ways to reach us

- **Email:** support@australware.io
- **Website:** https://australware.io/maid

## 🔒 Privacy

MaiD does not collect, store, or transmit any data. It's sandboxed with no network access —
your documents never leave your Mac. Issues here are public, so only share what you're
comfortable posting publicly.

## Requirements

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Universal binary — runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel.
